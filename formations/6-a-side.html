6-a-side Formations Explained

With five outfield players, 6-a-side lets you have a real defence, a midfield and an attack. Most teams play a 2-2-1 or a 2-1-2; pick the one that suits who turns up.

Open the free 6-a-side lineup builder

2-2-1

Two defenders, two midfielders and a striker. The balanced default.

Strengths
Every area of the pitch is covered and roles are easy to explain.
Watch out for
The striker has to work hard to press on their own.
Best for
Most pickup and league games.

1-3-1

A sweeper, three midfielders and a striker.

Strengths
Controls the middle of the pitch and keeps possession.
Watch out for
Wide defenders are missing, so the side midfielders must drop back.
Best for
Teams with good passers.
Use 1-3-1 in the lineup builder →

2-1-2

Two defenders, one central midfielder and two strikers.

Strengths
Two up top gives a constant goal threat.
Watch out for
The single midfielder gets outnumbered in the middle.
Best for
Attacking teams with a strong holding player.
Use 2-1-2 in the lineup builder →

1-2-2

One defender, two midfielders and two forwards.

Strengths
Lots of attacking options and a high press.
Watch out for
Thin at the back; counter-attacks hurt.
Best for
Chasing a game or playing a weaker side.
Use 1-2-2 in the lineup builder →

3-1-1

Three defenders, a midfielder and a striker.

Strengths
Solid and hard to score against.
Watch out for
Hard to create chances with only two players forward.
Best for
Protecting a lead.
