8-a-side Formations Explained

8-a-side sits between small-sided and full football: seven outfield players let you play with real width. The 2-3-2 and 3-2-2 are the most common choices.

Open the free 8-a-side lineup builder

2-3-2

Two defenders, three midfielders and two forwards.

Strengths
Strong in the middle with a front pair to finish moves.
Watch out for
Full-back areas are open, so wide midfielders must recover.
Best for
Balanced teams; a great default.

3-2-2

Three defenders, two midfielders and two forwards.

Strengths
Secure at the back while keeping two attackers.
Watch out for
Midfield can be outnumbered.
Best for
Teams that defend first and counter.

2-4-1

Two defenders, four midfielders and a striker.

Strengths
Controls possession and the width of the pitch.
Watch out for
The lone striker needs support from midfield runners.
Best for
Technical teams.

3-3-1

Three defenders, three midfielders and a striker.

Strengths
Very hard to break down and still solid in midfield.
Watch out for
Limited attacking numbers.
Best for
Facing stronger opposition.
Use 3-3-1 in the lineup builder →

2-2-3

Two defenders, two midfielders and a front three.

Strengths
Wide forwards stretch the defence and create chances.
Watch out for
Exposed through the middle on the counter.
Best for
Attacking teams with quick wingers.
