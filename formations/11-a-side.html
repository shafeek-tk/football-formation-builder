11-a-side Formations Explained

The full game has the most tactical variety. Here is every formation in the builder, from the classic 4-4-2 and modern 4-3-3 to historical shapes like the 2-3-5.

4-4-2

Four defenders, four midfielders, two strikers. The classic English formation.

Strengths
Simple, balanced and easy for any group to understand.
Watch out for
Can be outnumbered in central midfield.
Best for
Amateur and Sunday league teams.

4-3-3

A back four, three central midfielders and a front three.

Strengths
Width from the wingers and control in midfield.
Watch out for
Full-backs must cover the wide areas behind the wingers.
Best for
Possession teams; the most common elite shape.

4-2-3-1

Back four, two holding midfielders, three attacking midfielders and a striker.

Strengths
Defensive security with lots of creative players.
Watch out for
The lone striker needs strong support.
Best for
Balanced teams with a good number 10.

3-5-2

Three centre-backs, five midfielders including wing-backs, two strikers.

Strengths
Dominates midfield and gives width through the wing-backs.
Watch out for
Wing-backs need huge stamina.
Best for
Teams with two good strikers and fit wide players.

Best for
