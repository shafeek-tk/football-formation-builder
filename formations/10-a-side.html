10-a-side Formations Explained

10-a-side is common in leagues and pickup games when one player short of a full team. These shapes are the full-size classics with one player removed.

Open the free 10-a-side lineup builder

4-3-2

A back four, three midfielders and two strikers.

Strengths
Solid defence and a strike partnership.
Watch out for
Narrow without wingers, so full-backs must push on.
Best for
Most 10-a-side games.

3-4-2

Three defenders, four midfielders and two strikers.

Strengths
Strong in midfield with two up front.
Watch out for
Wing areas behind the midfield can be exposed.
Best for
Teams with fit wide midfielders.
Use 3-4-2 in the lineup builder →

4-2-3

A back four, two midfielders and a front three.

Strengths
Wide attackers and a striker give a constant threat.
Watch out for
Two midfielders can be outnumbered.
Best for
Attacking teams with quick wingers.
Use 4-2-3 in the lineup builder →

3-3-3

Three defenders, three midfielders and three forwards.

Strengths
Even across the pitch with lots of attacking options.
Watch out for
Gaps between the lines if players do not stay compact.
Best for
Open, high-scoring games.
